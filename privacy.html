Short version: Save A Life does not collect, store, or sell personal information.

What stays on your phone

Your chosen university and your lesson progress are saved only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app removes them.

What the app may request from the internet

To show verified campus information and up-to-date public statistics, the app may download content from our database. These requests are read-only and do not include your name, contact details, location, or any identifier we use to track you. As with any internet request, our hosting provider may briefly process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the response.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, logins, or sign-ups
  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No sale or sharing of personal data
  • No access to your contacts, photos, or location

Calls and links

When you tap a phone number or link, your phone opens the Phone app or your browser. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.

Questions: see our Support page.

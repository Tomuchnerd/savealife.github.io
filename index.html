In an emergency, call 911. For suicide or crisis support in the U.S., call or text 988. Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222.

What the app does

When to call for help. A quick check tells you whether to call 911, watch closely, or that things look normal, then walks you through the recovery position and Good Samaritan basics.
Crisis resources. One-tap access to 988, Crisis Text Line, Poison Control, and overdose steps.
University resources. Choose your school to see campus police, counseling, and alcohol-free activities.
Learning course. Short interactive lessons with saved progress and a BAC simulator.

Our approach

Save A Life does not support drinking, and especially not underage drinking. It exists to help people stay safe and help each other when something goes wrong. The app collects no personal data. See our privacy policy.

This app is educational and is not medical advice. It does not replace emergency services or professional care.
